What you can do

📚 Full catalog, every expansion

Browse every card across every set, starter deck, and promo, with current CardMarket prices.

🗂️ Collection tracking

Track exactly which cards you own — including playsets and duplicates — and how much your collection is worth.

💖 Wishlist

Keep a list of the cards you're chasing, right alongside your collection.

🔄 Trade lists, shareable by link

Mark cards you're willing to trade and share your list with another collector — no account needed on either side.

📈 Price analytics

See your collection's value over time, with your choice of price figure — trend, average, lowest listed, and more.

💾 Automatic backups

Your collection backs itself up automatically. Restore any snapshot, or export/import your data whenever you like.
